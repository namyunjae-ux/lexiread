How to Use LexiRead

Master global perspectives and elevate your English proficiency with 5 daily curated columns.
01

1. Read 5 Daily Curated Columns

Five high-caliber opinion and editorial columns from The Guardian are automatically published daily at midnight (00:00). Choose a column from the sidebar, read thoroughly, and click [Check] to fulfill your daily 5/5 reading goal.

02

2. Sentence Transcription & Typing Practice (Type Mode)

Click [Type & Study] on the toolbar to switch to interactive sentence transcription mode. Listen to native speech audio and type out each sentence to reinforce syntax, rhythm, and intuitive comprehension.

03

3. Double-Click for Native Audio Pronunciation

Curious about how a specific word sounds? Simply double-click (or double-tap on mobile) any word in the article body to instantly listen to its natural native English pronunciation.

04

4. Dedicated Per-Column Notes (Column Memo)

Click [Column Memo] to write personal reflections, key vocabulary, and summaries dedicated to each column. Switch smoothly between today's 5 columns using the top selector pills with real-time auto-saving.

05

5. Reading Calendar & Cloud Synchronization

Open [Calendar] to review past columns and track your reading consistency. [Sign In] to seamlessly synchronize your per-column memos and streak progress across all your devices.
